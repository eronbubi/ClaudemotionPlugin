---

If "$ARGUMENTS" names a style (standard, drawn, glass, funny) and the current folder has a `claudemotion.json`, set its "style" field to that value and confirm in one line.

Otherwise describe the four styles in one or two lines each, from `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/claudemotion/references/styles.md`:
- standard - Dribbble-level UI motion, warm gray, black and white, one accent, calm springs
- drawn - colored pencil on paper, wobbly boiling outlines, hatching, handwritten font, 24 fps feel
- glass - frosted glass panels over drifting colored light, calm and soft
- funny - loud colors, chunky outlines, bouncy squash and stretch, googly eyes, confetti, jokes in the copy
